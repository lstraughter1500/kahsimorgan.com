Meet Kahsi Morgan | Class of 2028

Driven by passion. Defined by character. Committed to excellence.

For Kahsi Morgan, success is about more than the scoreboard. It's about the work put in when no one is watching, the responsibility of leading others, and the determination to become better every day. A Class of 2028 student-athlete at Capital High School in Charleston, West Virginia, Kahsi is building his future through a combination of athletic talent, academic achievement, leadership, and an unwavering commitment to personal growth.

As a two-sport varsity starter and team captain in both basketball and soccer, Kahsi has embraced the challenge of competing, leading, and excelling in multiple arenas. Whether he's directing an offense, creating opportunities for his teammates, or setting the tone through his effort and attitude, he understands that leadership isn't simply about wearing a captain's title. It's about earning the trust of teammates, leading by example, and putting the team's success first.

A Competitor on the Court

Basketball is one of Kahsi's greatest passions. A left-handed combo guard, he brings versatility, court vision, and a competitive mindset to every possession. His ability to contribute in multiple roles reflects his understanding of the game and his willingness to do whatever his team needs to succeed.

Beyond competing for Capital High School, Kahsi continues to develop his game on the travel circuit with WV Hustle P32, gaining valuable experience against high-level competition. His dedication and performance have earned recognition, including 2026 Mountain State Athletic Conference (MSAC) All-Conference Special Honorable Mention and a No. 16 ranking among West Virginia's Class of 2028 basketball prospects by Prep Hoops.

For Kahsi, these accomplishments represent progress, not a finish line. His focus remains on sharpening his skills, expanding his understanding of the game, and preparing himself for the opportunity to compete at the collegiate level.

Versatility Beyond Basketball

Kahsi's athletic ability extends to the soccer field, where he serves as a team captain and primarily plays center attacking midfielder, while also contributing at winger and striker.

Competing in two demanding sports has helped shape him into a versatile athlete and a more complete leader. Soccer challenges his creativity, endurance, awareness, and ability to make quick decisions under pressure, qualities that translate directly to his performance on the basketball court.

More importantly, both sports have reinforced the values he carries into every competition: teamwork, accountability, resilience, and the willingness to compete for something bigger than himself.

Excellence in the Classroom

While athletics are an important part of Kahsi's life, his commitment to education is equally impressive.

Maintaining a 4.87 GPA while balancing two varsity sports, leadership responsibilities, and a demanding academic schedule reflects his discipline and dedication. As a National Honor Society member, Kahsi continues to challenge himself with an advanced curriculum that includes all honors courses, one dual-credit course, and one full college-level course.

His academic ambitions are closely connected to his passion for sports and helping others. Kahsi is interested in pursuing physical therapy in college, with the long-term goal of building a career that allows him to help individuals recover from injuries, regain confidence, improve performance, and return to doing what they love.

For Kahsi, pursuing physical therapy offers an opportunity to connect his understanding of athletics with a meaningful profession focused on making a difference in people's lives.

More Than an Athlete

What sets Kahsi apart isn't defined solely by statistics, rankings, awards, or academic achievements. It's the mindset behind them.

He is a student who embraces challenges, a teammate who values the success of others, and a young leader who recognizes that growth requires consistency, sacrifice, and hard work.

His goal is not simply to earn an opportunity to play college basketball, but to become the kind of student-athlete who makes a lasting impact on a college program, in the classroom, and in the community.

As he continues his journey toward the Class of 2028, Kahsi remains focused on what matters most: competing with purpose, leading with character, excelling academically, and making the most of every opportunity ahead.

The accomplishments tell part of his story. His character, ambition, and determination are what will shape the chapters still to come.
