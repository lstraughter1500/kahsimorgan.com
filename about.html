Bio

Kahsi Morgan

Kahsi Morgan is a Class of 2028 student athlete at Capital High School in Charleston, West Virginia. A starter and captain in both basketball and soccer, he balances the demands of two sports with a 4.87 GPA and a challenging academic schedule.

On the basketball court, Kahsi is a left-handed combo guard who also competes with WV Hustle P32. His 2026 recognition includes MSAC All-Conference Special Honorable Mention and a No. 16 ranking among West Virginia’s Class of 2028 players by Prep Hoops.

On the soccer field, Kahsi plays primarily as a center attacking midfielder, with the versatility to contribute at winger and striker. Competing across both sports gives him opportunities to develop his vision, decision-making, and ability to work with teammates in different game situations.

Kahsi brings that same commitment to the classroom. A National Honor Society student, he takes all honors courses, along with one dual credit course and one full college course. His focus is to keep improving as a student, athlete, and team captain while preparing for the next level.
